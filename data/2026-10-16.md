Motivation: 常规体探测手段无法将多带超导体的配对能隙分配到特定的电子或空穴费米面上，存在归属模糊问题。

Method: 结合比热与正常态磁输运约束，测量并分析热霍尔输运κ_xy，固定能隙大小与能带迁移率。

Result: 确定能隙为Δ_e/k_B≈13K、Δ_h/k_B≈8K，并完全确定κ_xx与κ_xy中的电子自由度；κ_xy在低场下的符号反转表明较大能隙位于重的Ce 4f电子片，较小能隙位于空穴片。

Conclusion: 热霍尔输运可有效解决多带超导体配对能隙的费米面归属歧义。

Abstract: Standard bulk probes of multiband superconductors cannot assign pairing gaps to specific electron or hole Fermi surfaces. Here we show that thermal Hall transport ($κ_{xy}$), constrained by specific heat and normal-state magnetotransport, resolves this ambiguity in the compensated heavy-fermion superconductor CeRu$_2$. Fixing the gap magnitudes ($Δ_e/k_B\approx 13$K, $Δ_h/k_B\approx 8$K) and band mobilities completely determines the electronic degrees of freedom in $κ_{xx}$ and $κ_{xy}$. A low-field sign reversal in $κ_{xy}$ places the larger gap on the heavy Ce $4f$ electron sheets and the smaller on the hole sheets.
