Motivation: 作者发现被评论文章中提出的"no-flow curves"实际上与DPY 2000年论文中已有的概念完全一致，存在原创性归属问题。文中声称该对象是"新的"，但其自身也承认DPY论文首次引入了时空局部守恒概念，这种自相矛盾的归因促使作者进行逐方程的对比验证，以澄清学术归因。

Method: 作者采用逐方程对比的方法，将被评论文章中的"no-flow curves"定义（dsigma/dt = H(u)/u）与DPY 2000年论文中的局部守恒欧拉-拉格朗日方法的时空积分曲线进行逐条比照，证明两者在数学上完全等价。同时利用全文语料库文献检索，追踪术语和归因的历史演变，并检查后续引用的准确性。

Result: 证明"no-flow curves"就是DPY积分曲线，"no-flow区域"就是该曲线所围成的管道。H'(u)和H(u)/u仅在通量函数为线性时恒等，而只有H(u)/u才能使横向质量通量消失。发现2025-2026年的论文在使用相同构造时引用较晚文献而未引用DPY原始工作，一篇2026年论文虽承认其为DPY积分曲线的"扩展"，但其零通量定义和比率常微分方程实际表明两者是同一对象。

Conclusion: 被评论文章中的"no-flow curves"并非新概念，而是DPY 2000年工作中已有的时空积分曲线。后续研究中存在系统性归因偏差，需要纠正。作者强调质疑的是连续曲线的原创性归属，而非后续离散贡献的有效性——后者并未受到挑战。

Abstract: This Comment concerns arXiv:2106.08363v3 [math.NA] by E. Abreu, A. Espirito Santo, W. Lambert and J. Perez, published in Numer. Methods Partial Differential Equations 39 (2023) 2400-2443. That article builds its scheme on space-time control volumes whose lateral boundaries, called "no-flow curves", solve dsigma/dt = H(u)/u and are presented as new. We show, equation by equation, that this object is the space-time integral curve of the locally conservative Eulerian-Lagrangian method of Douglas, Pereira and Yeh [Comput. Geosci. 4 (2000) 1-40], that the no-flow region is the tube those curves bound, and that its scalar forward-tracked form appears in Mancuso, Pereira and de Souza [TEMA 8 (2007) 269-276, 277-286]. At issue is not moving a control volume, which is generic, but which curve moves it: H'(u) and H(u)/u coincide identically only for a linear flux, and only the latter makes lateral mass flux vanish. The commented article itself calls the 2000 paper the first to introduce space-time local conservation, with an integral tube bounded by integral curves, while its abstract calls the same object introduced by the authors. A full-text corpus documents the changing terminology and attribution. Papers published in 2025 and 2026 use the same construction while citing later work but not DPY. Another 2026 paper gives mixed attribution: it calls the no-flow curve an "extension" of the DPY integral curve, although its own zero-flux definition and ratio ODE show identity, and it states that diffusion and dispersion do not modify the defining vector field. It extends the model, scheme and analysis, not the continuous curve. The later discrete contributions are not challenged.
