Finally, we study the combined SSH-AAH model to explore the interplay between the dimerized hopping and quasiperiodicity. The results show that the SSH dimerization determines the oscillatory behavior and penetration of the induced pairing, while the AAH potential enhances spatial inhomogeneity and further reduces its magnitude. Together, these two effects provide a versatile means of controlling proximity-induced superconductivity in quasiperiodic hybrid systems.
